# SPECTRA v2: recipe and full results

This page gives the full v2 recipe, the stop rule, and per-backbone results next to the frozen
base model and the paper's checkpoints. The [README](../README.md) has a shorter summary.

## Recipe

| | paper (`main`) | v2 (this branch) |
|---|---|---|
| data | PLISM, 2 conditions × 900 locations per step | PLISM, 2 conditions × 256 locations per step, 224 px |
| loss | InfoNCE with pseudo-tissue reweighting (same-group negatives up-weighted by e^3 in the CLS loss, dropped from the GeM loss) | symmetric InfoNCE, τ = 0.1, no same-core bias (other locations of the same core are ordinary negatives) |
| adapter | LoRA r32 / α64, attention + MLP | LoRA r16 / α32, attention + MLP |
| heads | CLS + GeM | CLS 0.5 + GeM 0.5, each Linear-BN-GELU-Linear → 512 |
| schedule | lr 1e-4, 200-step linear warmup, 500 steps, checkpoint every 50 | AdamW, lr 1e-4, wd 0.05, 200-step warmup + cosine over 400 steps, truncated at the stop step |
| stop step | tolerance rule (ε = 0.007) on the **PathoROB RI** curve, per seed | **R1** on held-out PLISM retrieval of one selection run, per backbone (below) |

The GeM head is a softplus generalized mean over the patch tokens (register tokens excluded),
with one learnable exponent p (init 3). Only the LoRA weights, the GeM exponent and the two
projectors are trained.

### Stop rule R1

1. Train a **selection run** (seed 1) with the GT450 scanner, the MY stain and 7 of the 46 tissue
   cores held out: 72 of the 91 PLISM conditions, and no location of a held-out core, reach a
   training batch.
2. Every 25 steps, score held-out PLISM retrieval P on the 7 held-out cores imaged under every
   condition (except the defocused HRH_S60). P = ½ · same-glass cross-scanner top-1 retrieval +
   ½ · CRoMa on the PLISM tiles (mean of its median and lower-tail mean), averaged over the CLS
   and CLS+mean readouts (`spectra.sweep.evaluate`).
3. Stop step = argmax over s ≥ 25 of P(s), ties to the earlier step (`spectra.sweep.stopping`).
4. Train the release model on **all 91 conditions and all cores** with the same schedule,
   stopped at that step (seeds 0, 1, 2).

No labels and no test benchmark are read at any point. P is a PLISM metric only.

| backbone | Virchow2 | UNI2-h | H-optimus-0 | Virchow | Phikon-v2 | Midnight-12k | OpenMidnight-1.1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 stop step | 100 | 175 | 100 | 225 | 225 | 225 | 225 |

## Results

* **base**: the frozen backbone, scored with the same evaluation code as v2. It can differ in the
  third decimal from the base values in the paper.
* **paper**: the paper's checkpoints (3 seeds, each at its PathoROB-selected step).
  PathoROB RI and CRoMa were re-scored for this page with the same evaluation code and
  preprocessing as v2 (CRoMa is not in the paper). The paper's own RI used a different
  preprocessing (fp16, bicubic resize 256 → center crop 224); its reported values are
  Virchow2 0.9091, UNI2-h 0.9074, H-optimus-0 0.9055, Virchow 0.8904, Phikon-v2 0.8356,
  Midnight-12k 0.9082, OpenMidnight 0.8794. HEST and THUNDER are the paper's reported numbers.
  For UNI2-h and H-optimus-0 the current evaluation code reproduces them on the paper's
  checkpoints (HEST 0.4237 / 0.4225; THUNDER within 0.2 points, segmentation 69.0 / 65.2); for
  the other backbones this has not been re-checked, so small protocol differences are possible.
* **CPTAC**: the paper's values are shown only for UNI2-h (0.6980) and H-optimus-0 (0.6895),
  where the current pipeline reproduces them exactly from the paper's checkpoints. For the other
  backbones the paper's CPTAC (on `main`) was not re-verified in the current pipeline, so it is
  shown as "–".
* **v2**: 3-seed mean ± SD of the all-data release runs at the R1 step. `n=` marks cells with
  fewer than 3 seeds. "pending" means the evaluation is still running.
* PathoROB RI is the mean robustness index over Camelyon, TCGA and Tolkach-ESCA. CRoMa
  (PathoROB features) is reported as the median and the mean of the lowest 10% (LTM10); higher is
  better for both. HEST is the mean Pearson r over 9 tasks; CPTAC is the mean macro one-vs-rest AUC over
  its classification tasks; THUNDER is in %.
* The paper picked each checkpoint by PathoROB RI, so its RI (and to a lesser extent CRoMa) is
  optimistically biased. v2 picks its step without any test benchmark.

### Summary

* **CRoMa median**: v2 is higher than the paper on all 7 backbones.
* **PathoROB RI** (same protocol for both): v2 is higher on UNI2-h (+0.008), Virchow (+0.010)
  and Midnight-12k (+0.012), and on OpenMidnight (+0.012, different base model). It is lower on
  H-optimus-0 (-0.002), Virchow2 (-0.002) and Phikon-v2 (-0.009). The paper selected its
  checkpoints on this metric; v2 does not.
* **CRoMa LTM10**: the paper is better on UNI2-h, H-optimus-0, Virchow2 and Phikon-v2; v2 is
  better on Virchow, Midnight-12k and OpenMidnight.
* **HEST**: v2 is higher on Virchow2 (+0.002), within 0.002 of the paper on UNI2-h, H-optimus-0
  and Virchow, and lower on Midnight-12k (-0.003) and Phikon-v2 (-0.004).
* **CPTAC**: v2 improves over the frozen base on every backbone measured. Against the paper
  (verified for two backbones only) it is higher on UNI2-h (+0.003) and lower on H-optimus-0
  (-0.004).
* **THUNDER** (available for Virchow2 with 2 seeds, UNI2-h, H-optimus-0): v2 is at or above the paper on
  Virchow2 and H-optimus-0; on UNI2-h it is equal on kNN and segmentation, +0.3 on linear and
  -0.4 on 16-shot.
* Against the frozen base, v2 improves RI, CRoMa LTM10, HEST and CPTAC on every backbone where
  they have been measured. CRoMa median also improves except on Midnight-12k, whose frozen base
  already scores 0.363 (above both tuned versions).
