# THUNDER seed floor on the FULL 12-dataset task mean

Generated by `scripts/thunder_seed_floor_12ds.py`; raw numbers in `docs/thunder_seed_floor_12ds.json`.
Metric f1, adaptation=frozen, THUNDER root `/data/ryan.kim/thunder/outputs/res`.

## Why this supersedes `docs/thunder_seed_floor.md` for reported numbers

`docs/thunder_seed_floor.md` measured the floor on a task mean over **5** datasets
(bach, mhist, break_his, bracs, ccrcc) from a **single seed pair** (`fast5_ctrl` vs
`fast5_ctrlseed`). Every THUNDER classification number we actually report is a task
mean over the **12** `PAPER_CLS` datasets (`scripts/collect_final5.py:102-113`). A
12-dataset mean averages more per-dataset noise, so the published 5-dataset floor is
too large for the quantity being graded, and it forced cells to read UNRESOLVABLE
that are in fact resolvable. It also had **no floor at all for `simple_shot`**.

This document measures the floor directly on the 12-dataset mean, and on the same 5
datasets with the same seeds, so the ratio is apples-to-apples.

## Statistical definition -- identical to `scripts/thunder_seed_floor.py`

For a pair of seed replicates (a, b) and a dataset list of size n:

```
d_ds        = f1(b, ds) - f1(a, ds)
centred_2se = 2*SD(d)/sqrt(n)                 # LEGACY, prices only the part that averages away
offset_2se  = |mean(d)| + 2*SD(d)/sqrt(n)     # RECOMMENDED (unchanged from the old doc)
```

`n` is the number of DATASETS, exactly as before. With 5 seeds there are 10 unordered
pairs instead of 1, so we report the **mean** offset_2se over the 10 pairs (the direct
analogue of the single published number) and the max (conservative reading).

We also report a second, more stable bar that uses all 5 seeds jointly:

```
seed_sd      = SD across the 5 seeds of the 12-dataset task mean
pairdiff_2sd = 2*sqrt(2)*seed_sd     # 2-sigma bar on the difference of two single runs
```

## Why the ratio beats sqrt(5/12), and why that is not a free lunch

Two things were checked rather than assumed.

**1. Is the per-dataset seed noise correlated across datasets?** Essentially no. Mean
pairwise Pearson r of the per-seed deviations across the 66 dataset pairs, per cell:

| cell | mean cross-dataset r |
|---|---|
| phikon/knn | +0.048 |
| phikon/linear_probing | -0.039 |
| phikon/simple_shot | +0.213 |
| midnight/knn | +0.004 |
| midnight/linear_probing | -0.020 |
| midnight/simple_shot | +0.038 |
| virchow2/knn | +0.136 |
| virchow2/linear_probing | +0.048 |
| virchow2/simple_shot | +0.097 |

So the sqrt(n) averaging argument is not defeated by correlation here. That is a
measured result, not an assumption -- had r been large, the reduction would not have
materialised and this whole document would have been void.

**2. Why then is the reduction better than sqrt(n)?** Because the 5-dataset panel is not
a random subset: it is exactly the five noisiest datasets. Across-seed SD per dataset,
averaged over the 9 backbone/task cells:

| dataset | mean across-seed SD | in the 5ds panel? |
|---|---|---|
| break_his | 0.0205 | YES |
| mhist | 0.0104 | YES |
| bach | 0.0096 | YES |
| bracs | 0.0079 | YES |
| ccrcc | 0.0074 | YES |
| esca | 0.0059 | no |
| tcga_uniform | 0.0054 | no |
| tcga_crc_msi | 0.0052 | no |
| patch_camelyon | 0.0033 | no |
| wilds | 0.0031 | no |
| crc | 0.0025 | no |
| tcga_tils | 0.0023 | no |

The five panel datasets occupy the top five slots. The extra seven datasets
(crc, tcga_tils, wilds, patch_camelyon, tcga_crc_msi, tcga_uniform, esca) are 2-8x
quieter, so adding them both increases n and lowers the average per-dataset variance.
The 5-dataset floor was therefore doubly wrong for a 12-dataset mean: wrong n, and
wrong noise level.

## Comparison to the published 5-dataset floor

The published numbers came from a *different run family* (`fast5_ctrl` vs
`fast5_ctrlseed`, a different recipe) and from a **single pair, i.e. 2 seeds**. Our
recomputation on the same 5 datasets but with the final5 family and 10 pairs gives
systematically different values -- e.g. phikon/cls/knn 0.0548 here vs 0.0297 published.
Neither is 'the' floor for the other family; what transfers is the *ratio*, which is
computed within a single family and is therefore not affected by this offset.

| published cell | published 5ds offset-2SE (n=2 seeds) | final5 5ds offset-2SE (n=5 seeds) |
|---|---|---|
| cls/knn (via phikon/knn) | 0.0297 | 0.0548 |
| cls/linear_probing (via phikon/linear_probing) | 0.0156 | 0.0226 |
| clsmean/knn (via midnight/knn) | 0.0468 | 0.0221 |
| clsmean/linear_probing (via midnight/linear_probing) | 0.0208 | 0.0142 |
| clsmean/knn (via virchow2/knn) | 0.0468 | 0.0168 |
| clsmean/linear_probing (via virchow2/linear_probing) | 0.0208 | 0.0178 |

**Confidence.** Every cell here rests on n=5 seeds (10 pairs), df=4 for the SD.
The published doc rested on n=2 seeds (1 pair, df=0 across seeds), so it is the
*old* number that is low-confidence, not these. No cell here falls below n=4.

### Plain statement

Under the corrected 12-dataset floor, **six of the nine cells become gradeable**:

- **Comfortably resolvable (ratio < 0.5):** midnight/simple_shot (0.28) and
  virchow2/simple_shot (0.24). Both are large Waiv gains against a small floor.
- **Resolvable but marginal (0.5 <= ratio < 1):** phikon/simple_shot (0.58),
  midnight/knn (0.59), phikon/knn (0.63), phikon/linear_probing (0.69). These are
  gradeable, but a single-seed arm sitting at half the Waiv gain cannot be distinguished
  from one sitting at the full gain. Grade them from the 5-seed mean, not one run.
- **Still UNRESOLVABLE:** midnight/linear_probing (4.37), virchow2/knn (2.76),
  virchow2/linear_probing (2.93). In all three, Waiv's own claimed gain (+0.002, -0.003,
  +0.003) is smaller than our seed noise on the same benchmark. These cells cannot be
  graded by any amount of care on our side; the effect Waiv reports is below the
  measurement resolution of the instrument. Do not report a verdict for them.

**Change vs the old floor.** Under the published 5-dataset floor, only
midnight/simple_shot and virchow2/simple_shot had any floor at all (`simple_shot` was
ungraded), and of the six cells that did have one, phikon/knn (0.0297 vs 0.037) and
phikon/linear_probing (0.0156 vs 0.014) read borderline/unresolvable. The correction
newly opens three `simple_shot` cells (which had no floor) and firms up phikon/knn and
phikon/linear_probing. It does not rescue any of the three genuinely-unresolvable cells.
