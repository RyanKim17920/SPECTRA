# THUNDER on Waiv's 16-dataset classification roster

Date: 2026-08-26.  Supersedes the 12-dataset THUNDER numbers in
`docs/FORMULA_UNIFICATION_2026-08-26.md`.  Generated from
`docs/final_recipe_verdict.json` (16-set) and `docs/final_recipe_verdict_12ds.json` (12-set).

## The defect

Waiv (arXiv:2607.22861 Table 2) average THUNDER **classification** over **16** datasets:
the THUNDER paper's 12, plus the 4 SPIDER organ subsets (breast / colorectal / skin /
thorax), which postdate the THUNDER paper and have no segmentation task.  We averaged
over **12**.

`pct_of_waiv = (ours - our_base) / (waiv_ft - waiv_base)`.  If our base is a mean over a
different dataset panel than theirs, the numerator carries a constant offset that has
nothing to do with the recipe.  That is what a ~2-point base gap was.

## Which side of the ratio moved

Only OUR numerator.  Waiv's per-task gains are their published figures, and those
figures are already 16-set means (that is exactly what the base agreement above
demonstrates -- our 16-set base reproduces their published base, so their published
base is a 16-set mean, and their FT number is the same panel).  The denominator
`waiv_ft - waiv_base` is therefore UNCHANGED by this fix.  What changed is that our
base and our fine-tuned score are now measured on the same panel Waiv measured on.

## Degenerate denominators: do they survive the roster change?

**Yes, unchanged -- because they live in the DENOMINATOR, which the roster does not
touch.**  Waiv's published per-task gains are what they are:

| cell | Waiv gain (raw) | note |
|---|---|---|
| phikon/knn | +0.0370 | well conditioned |
| phikon/linear_probing | +0.0140 | well conditioned |
| phikon/simple_shot | +0.0150 | well conditioned |
| midnight/knn | +0.0170 | well conditioned |
| midnight/linear_probing | +0.0020 | below the measured seed SD (0.0021) |
| midnight/simple_shot | +0.0370 | well conditioned |
| virchow2/knn | -0.0030 | NEGATIVE -- Waiv REGRESSED |
| virchow2/linear_probing | +0.0030 | well conditioned |
| virchow2/simple_shot | +0.0270 | well conditioned |

This is exactly why the report pools the three tasks by RATIO OF MEANS (one numerator,
one denominator, divided once) instead of averaging three per-task percentages.  A
per-task ratio divides by that task's own gain; `virchow2/knn`'s gain is NEGATIVE, so
its per-task ratio rewards regressing, and `midnight/linear_probing`'s +0.0020 gain
produces a 522% per-task figure.  Pooled, virchow2's denominator is +0.0090 and
midnight's is +0.0187 -- both real scales.  The per-task figures remain in the report
as an appendix and are graded by nothing.

## Caveats that must travel with these numbers

1. **Segmentation is still 0/4.** No SPIDER set has a segmentation task, and the two
   segpath cells are not run on this cohort.  "THUNDER" in this report means
   **classification only**, on 3 of Waiv's 6 THUNDER tasks (knn / linear / few-shot).
2. **The seed floors are still 12-dataset measurements.**  `THUNDER_FLOOR` and
   `THUNDER_SEED_SD` come from a 5-seed cohort scored on 12 datasets.  A 16-dataset
   mean averages over MORE datasets and is therefore LESS noisy, so reusing the 12ds SD
   as the error floor OVER-states the error bar.  That is the conservative direction.
   It is reused rather than rescaled because no 5-seed SPIDER cohort exists to measure
   with; rescaling would be a tuned constant.
3. **n=2 on the THUNDER cells.**  The CI cohort is seeds 0 and 1 only
   (`f5_ci-<bb>-s{0,1}-<jobid>`).  virchow2's RI and HEST cells are n=5.

## What changed in code

- `scripts/collect_final5.py` is the single owner of the classification roster:
  `PAPER_CLS_THUNDER12`, `PAPER_CLS_SPIDER`, `PAPER_CLS_WAIV16`.  `PAPER_CLS` stays the
  12-set panel so existing consumers (seed-floor measurement, scoreboard,
  thunder_seed_delta) keep reading the quantity they were built against.
- `scripts/collect_thunder.py` imports the roster instead of redefining it, the same
  single-owner pattern already used for `PAPER_SEG`.
- `scripts/final_recipe_report.py` gains `--cls-roster {12,16}`, default **16**;
  `--cls-roster 12` reproduces every pre-change number.  It also gains
  `thunder_base_gap()`, which emits the table above, and a PER-MODEL criterion block.
